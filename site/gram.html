FAQ

GRAM questions.

Which wallets do I need?
Any VIZ wallet, and any GRAM wallet that supports Jettons (e.g. Tonkeeper).
How long does bridging take?
Peg-in ~40s plus the mint. Peg-out a few seconds plus the release — usually under a minute.
Why does peg-out ask for ~0.1 GRAM?
That covers network gas for the Jetton transfer; the unused excess is refunded to your wallet.
