FAQ

Solana questions.

Which wallets do I need?
Any VIZ wallet, and any Solana wallet — Phantom, Solflare, a hardware wallet, or an exchange withdrawal all work. This site never asks to connect your wallet.
Why a personal deposit address?
Peg-out uses a deposit address derived for your VIZ account. Anything sent to it can only ever be released to that account — there is no memo to get wrong.
How long does bridging take?
Peg-in about a minute. Peg-out: your VIZ is released after the Solana transfer finalizes — usually a minute or two.
